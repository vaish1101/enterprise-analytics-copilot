# Limitations

- Coverage is limited to reviewed Mercedes-Benz, Henkel and Siemens reporting
  periods, scopes and catalog metrics. These companies are not clients or endorsers.
- Company-specific measures are not automatically comparable. Matching units or
  successful reconciliation does not establish semantic equivalence.
- Quarter, half-year, year and fiscal-period grain cannot be freely substituted.
  Missing supported context should clarify or refuse.
- Management-reported contributions are attribution, not causal proof. The
  Mercedes combined volume/structure/net-pricing category remains unsplit.
- Source extraction, evidence tagging and catalog curation can be wrong despite
  valid downstream arithmetic. Verification is not universal truth checking.
- Numerical prose validation intentionally rejects unsupported formats. This can
  cause conservative revisions, supported subsets or refusals.
- The public-safe Docker demo contains reviewed derived figures and a template
  provider, not the local documentary corpus or a live-model integration. It
  deliberately refuses documentary questions.
- No arbitrary SQL/Python execution, forecasting contract or prompt-only company
  onboarding is implemented. Analysts remain responsible for interpretation.
- The API has no validated enterprise authentication, tenant isolation or
  authorized per-user session-access boundary. Do not expose it as an enterprise
  production service merely because Docker starts.
- Anonymous request quota is per process and transport peer, not distributed
  identity. NAT clients may share quota. Restarts reset it; multiple workers or
  replicas need shared gateway controls. IP rotation is not comprehensively prevented.
- A request timeout does not prove background threaded work was cancelled.
  Queue/backpressure and spending lifecycle remain deployment review items.
- Live-provider reliability/cost depends on configuration and failed-attempt
  visibility. Unknown/unconfigured price coefficients are not a zero-cost claim.
- The frontend-only public mode is recorded replay, not live question answering.
  Questions outside its selected examples do not trigger backend analysis.
  German controls do not imply translation of English analytical findings.
- No public deployment, hosted live demo, horizontal-scaling benchmark or
  unrestricted enterprise readiness is claimed.
